# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
* Using welcoming and inclusive language
* Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences

## Enforcement Responsibilities
Community leaders are responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable behavior.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces.

## Enforcement
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported.

## Enforcement Guidelines
Community leaders will follow these Community Impact Guidelines in determining consequences.
